Process Workflow

How CivicFix Enforces Working-Day Deadlines

Every citizen filing is routed to the responsible Bengaluru body. The clock excludes Karnataka holidays and weekends.

01

Geotag & Submit

Upload photo evidence, landmark (e.g. 100ft Road, 4th Main), and category. A reference ID like BLR-2026-01 is generated.

Instant Ref Code
02

Department Routing

Auto-routed to City Corporation (roads), BESCOM (lighting), BWSSB (water/sewage), or Solid Waste Agency with assigned field supervisor.

Zonal Dispatch
03

Working-Day SLA Clock

The statutory countdown starts. Countdown timer automatically skips Karnataka government holidays and weekends (Saturdays and Sundays).

Holiday-Aware Clock
04

Escalation & Sign-Off

Breached deadlines trigger escalation to Zonal Joint Commissioners. On completion, post-repair photo requires resident confirmation.

Resident Sign-Off

Issue Categories, Responsible Bodies & Working-Day SLAs

Resolution limits in working days. Note: Deadlines skip Karnataka government holidays and weekends (Saturdays and Sundays).

SLA: 2 Working Days City Corporation

Potholes & Footpath Damage

Severe asphalt craters, monsoon rain washout, unpaved utility cuts, broken footpath pavers, and open drain slabs across arterial and cross roads.

Authority: City Corporation / Civic Body
Escalation: Executive Road Engineer → Zonal Commissioner
On-time (Sample): 93.4% redressed
Report Pothole
SLA: 1 Working Day Solid Waste Agency

Garbage & Black Spots

Uncollected roadside refuse, missed wet/dry segregated waste collection, commercial garbage dumps, and street-corner black spots.

Authority: Solid Waste Management Agency
Escalation: Ward Marshal → Health Inspector
On-time (Sample): 96.8% redressed
Report Waste
SLA: 3 Working Days BESCOM

Streetlights & BESCOM Faults

Dark street lamps, flickering neighborhood fixtures, dangling service cables, and damaged distribution boxes.

Authority: BESCOM (Electrical Grid)
Escalation: Sub-Division AEE → Executive Engineer
On-time (Sample): 92.1% redressed
Report Streetlight
SLA: 1 Working Day (12h Leak) BWSSB

Water Leaks & Sewage Overflow

Cauvery drinking water pipeline bursts, overflowing manholes, blocked underground drainage (UGD), and low-pressure contaminated supply.

Authority: BWSSB (Water & Sewerage)
Escalation: Assistant Executive Engineer → Chief Hydro
On-time (Sample): 90.5% redressed
