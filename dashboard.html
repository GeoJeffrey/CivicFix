Statutory Working-Days Calculation Protocol

Under municipal rules, the resolution countdown strictly counts official working hours (09:00 to 17:30 IST) across working days only. The clock automatically pauses on 2nd and 4th Saturdays, all Sundays, and notified Karnataka state public holidays. Escalations trigger automatically when working-day thresholds lapse without verified photographic closure.
